Before you record

  • Wear wired earphones so the other voice doesn't leak into your mic. Bluetooth earbud mics are low quality; the mic check warns you if one is in use.
  • Hold or prop the phone 15–20 cm (6–8 in) from your mouth, a little to the side, so you talk across its bottom mic, not straight into it (that stops "p" and "b" pops).
  • Pick a quiet, soft room (sofa, curtains, carpet). Turn off fans and AC. Keep the phone still and don't let fingers rub the bottom edge.
  • Run the mic check. It tells you the right distance for your voice.

Help: how a call works
  1. Both phones open this page in Chrome and type a name.
  2. One taps Start a new call. A 4-letter code shows up. Text it to your friend.
  3. The other types the code and taps Join call. Both screens say Connected, the friend card turns green, and you hear each other.
  4. Either taps Start recording. Both phones count down 3-2-1 and record. The friend card shows that their phone is recording too.
  5. Either taps Stop & save. Each phone saves its own file and lists it under Recordings.
Won't connect? Put both phones on Wi-Fi (some mobile networks block direct calls). Check the code and keep the first phone on the call screen. If the call drops, recording keeps going and the page reconnects by itself. Try Record solo to see the recording screen without a friend.
